01 / Kamish

KAMISH

A multi-region event broker that keeps data inside its legal regions, routes around bad links, and survives a regional outage. Data stays where the law says it can.

See how it works
02 / The problem

Three requirements that pull against each other

Residency

Data stays inside its permitted regions.

Adaptivity

Replication routes around slow or degraded links.

Survivability

A regional outage degrades one data class, not the whole service.

03 / Residency

One decision function guards every exit

Every point that stores or sends payload asks the same signed policy. Restricted data aimed at Region C is denied. Open data passes.

■ Restricted   ■ Open
04 / Adaptive routing

Reroute where the law allows

When A to B degrades, open traffic detours via C. Restricted traffic has no legal alternate, so it waits in a bounded outbox. Policy is a hard filter, never a score.

05 / Failover

A falls. B takes over under a new epoch

Epoch 7

Promotion needs policy eligibility and a quorum-committed epoch. When A returns, its stale writes are fenced and moved to quarantine.

06 / Evidence

Proof is a scan, not a claim

A unique canary record per class is scanned for in forbidden regions after every fault scenario.
