The Multi-Region Challenge

Three Unforgiving Hazards

Traditional event brokers assume unified network fidelity and boundary-free replication. In modern sovereign infrastructure, all three fail simultaneously.

HAZARD 01
Residency
Accidental cross-border packet egress triggers irreversible sovereign compliance breaches.
HAZARD 02
Adaptivity
Transatlantic cables degrade with 300ms+ jitter, backing up outbox queues and stalling local producers.
HAZARD 03
Survivability
Sudden regional blackout produces split-brain ghost commits unless strict monotonic leases fence old leaders.
Zero-Egress Guard · Gate 2
SEND.DENY ENFORCEMENT

Residency Enforcement

One decision function guards every point where data is stored or sent.

Crimson packets sent toward Region C hit a scale-like barrier at its boundary, flash, and burn out with a send.deny label. Ice-blue packets pass through unimpeded.

In the tested scenarios · Zero egress violations
WAN Dynamics · Gate 3
DYNAMIC REROUTE ACTIVE

Adaptive Routing

"Policy is a hard filter, never a score."

The A–B link degrades (turns red, packets slow and jitter). Ice-blue traffic reroutes via Region C. Crimson traffic holds at Region A because there is no legal alternate.

In the tested scenarios · Zero leaks during link degradation
Survivability · Gate 4
EPOCH LEASE: 7

Failover

Region A goes dark. Region B is promoted: its platform brightens and an epoch counter changes 7 → 8.

When A returns, a stale epoch-7 packet is rejected and diverted into a small quarantine box.

In the tested scenarios · Zero split-brain commits
