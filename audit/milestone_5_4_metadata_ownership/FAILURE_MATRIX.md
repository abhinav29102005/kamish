# KAMISH MILESTONE 5.4: FAILURE MATRIX
## Systematic Failure Analysis of Metadata & Ownership Operations

**Baseline Commit:** `27cbc49fd14f32f71a8be513b0eca7f81b796ebc`  
**Scope:** Distributed metadata authority, partition ownership state machine, lease lifecycle, and disk epoch fencing.

---

| ID | Failure Mode | Component(s) | Pre-Condition | Observed System Behavior | Risk Rating | Correctness Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **F01** | Lease expires while WAL append is in flight | `BrokerServer`, `PartitionLog` | Broker lease valid at start of produce; expires during disk `sync_all`. | Append commits to disk, but post-append check detects `now >= lease_expiry_ns` and returns `Err(PolicyDenied("lease_expired_outcome_unknown"))`. | **F02** | Stale owner attempts produce after epoch bump | `BrokerServer`, `EtcdMetadataAuthority` | Partition ownership reassigned to Region B at epoch E+1. | **F03** | Concurrent CAS race for partition ownership | `EtcdMetadataAuthority` | Region A and Region B concurrently propose epoch E -> E+1. | Single atomic etcd Txn compares `epoch_key == E`. | **F04** | Complete metadata cluster cold restart | `EtcdMetadataAuthority`, `etcd 3.7.3` | Epoch 42 committed. | **F05** | In-memory `MetadataStore` restart with empty state | `MetadataStore`, `BrokerServer` | In-memory store restarted; partition previously written at epoch 5. | In `SingleNodeCompatibility`, defaults to epoch 1. | **F06** | Network partition between broker and authority | `BrokerServer`, `EtcdMetadataAuthority` | Broker partitioned from etcd cluster. | In `MultiRegion` mode, `kv_client.get` fails; `get_ownership` returns `None`; broker rejects write requests (`unregistered_stream_in_multi_region_mode`). | **MEDIUM** (Availability) | Broker safely fails closed, but availability is lost even while local monotonic lease may still be physically valid. |
| **F07** | Quorum loss in consensus cluster (< 2/3 alive) | `EtcdMetadataAuthority`, `etcd 3.7.3` | Two of three etcd members killed (`ap-south` and `eu-central`). | Etcd Txn and read RPCs block/fail; `EtcdMetadataAuthority` enters error state; broker stops admitting writes. | **F09** | System wall-clock backward step (NTP jump) | `BrokerServer::produce` | Active lease valid for 30s. | **F10** | Ambiguous commit during network disconnect | `EtcdMetadataAuthority` | Region A submits CAS E -> E+1. Network drops before response received. | **F11** | Direct CAS takeover without waiting for lease expiry | `BrokerServer::claim_partition_ownership` | Region A holds lease expiring at T+60s. | **F12** | Local WAL max epoch differs from authority epoch | `PartitionLog`, `BrokerServer` | Region A appended records up to epoch 3. Region B took over at epoch 4.
